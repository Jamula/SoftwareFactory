# Native merge queue readiness and PR merge latency SLO (#4, #8, #15, #16)

This change adds merge_group checks_requested triggers to existing candidate validation workflows. PRs still validate their exact heads; merge groups validate the exact combined SHA. Keep every existing test, component/overall coverage threshold, pinned action, and permission boundary. The reusable smoke still replays its pinned fixture; it is not candidate validation. No queue or branch protection is enabled here.

Before enabling a queue, inventory actual required contexts and trusted producers in the main ruleset, including any workflows merged after this change. Verify each external check supports merge groups; do not replace an independent PR approval with a group check. In a separately approved configuration change, keep squash, required reviews, conversation resolution, security and existing quality gates. Begin with one build lane, verify included capacity, then increase only with observed throughput and unchanged safety. Keep merge-group and PR validation evidence distinct. Validate a real group and main before claiming operational queue support. If a required check cannot report against the group, stop and resolve the integration rather than bypass it.

Measure queue admission to last merge receipt and separately main verification; report the 120-second target as a target, not a result. GitHub documents the required event at https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/configuring-branches-and-merges-in-your-repository/configuring-pull-request-merges/managing-a-merge-queue .

## Deterministic protected squash gate (`#8`)

SoftwareFactory retains its deterministic merge-eligibility moat (`tools/engineering-factory/core.mjs`, `tools/engineering-factory/protected-squash.mjs`, `tools/engineering-factory/platform-merge-gates.mjs`, and `node tools/engineering-factory/run.mjs --evaluate-squash`) alongside **GitHub Native Squash Merge Queue** for final `main` integration (`gh-aw` `merge-pull-request` intentionally refuses default-branch merges):

- **Parameterized repository scope (`#13` / `#8`):** `guardedSquashEvidence`, `readProtectedSquashCandidate`, `createGitHubShadowTransport`, `createShadowSquashController`, and `platformMergeGates` default to `DEFAULT_FACTORY_SCOPE` (`Jamula/SoftwareFactory`, repository ID `1471342715`, branch `main`) while accepting explicit consumer `scope` and trusted `policy` inputs, failing closed (`PROTECTIONS_UNVERIFIED` / `PROVENANCE_MISMATCH`) on repository, ID, or branch mismatches.
- **Exact-head quality, security, and review evidence:** Candidates must satisfy `>=98%` line and `>=95%` branch coverage per SoftwareFactory executable component and overall (preserving stricter consumer floors), trusted exact-head required checks (`build-test`, `upload-coverage-javascript`, `windows-aspire-runtime`, `CodeQL`), resolved inline review threads, fresh compatible base, and healthy `main`.
- **Independent review semantics:** For `Jamula/SoftwareFactory` where the `main` ruleset sets `required_approving_review_count: 0` with a verified hosted review gate, a completed independent Copilot `COMMENTED` review on the exact current head is accepted only when every inline and summary-only material finding is dispositioned with zero unresolved concerns and zero blocking reviews. Stale, dismissed, pending, or self-authored reviews block fail-closed; consumer policies requiring formal `APPROVED` counts retain their stricter thresholds.
- **Serialized shadow integration and `main` recovery:** `createShadowSquashController` serializes candidate evaluation, holds uncertain outcomes in recovery (`SERIALIZED_OR_RECOVERY_REQUIRED` / `RECOVERY_REQUIRED`), re-reads authoritative receipts, verifies post-merge `main` health (`SHADOW_MAIN_VERIFIED`) before allowing dependent work, and keeps `liveMerge: false` until separately authorized.

## Owner-directed PR merge latency SLO & stage-delay attribution (`#8`)

`tools/engineering-factory/merge-queue-slo.mjs` (`evaluatePullMergeLatencySlo(samples, options)` and `evaluatePullMergeLatencySample(sample)`) evaluates PR lifecycle velocity and stage-level waiting purely from complete, trusted timeline and creation-state metadata:

| Lifecycle metric | Primary SLO target | Stretch target | Measurement rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `draftToPublishMs` | p50 `<= 180,000 ms` (`3 min`) | `<= 60,000 ms` (`1 min`) | PR creation to **first** `ready_for_review`; `0` **only** when explicit verified creation-state evidence proves `createdAsDraft === false` (`updated_at` / `updatedAt` is rejected fail-closed as `UPDATED_AT_FORBIDDEN`) |
| `publishToMergeMs` | p50 `<= 420,000 ms` (`7 min`) | `<= 180,000 ms` (`3 min`) | **First** publication to squash merge receipt |
| `creationToMergeMs` | p50 `<= 600,000 ms` (`10 min`) | `<= 240,000 ms` (`4 min`) | PR creation to squash merge receipt (supersedes the earlier 15-minute objective while retaining separate `main` verification) |
| `eligibleToMergeMs` | p95 `<= 120,000 ms` (`2 min`) | `<= 30,000 ms` (`30 s`) | **First** verified merge-eligibility timestamp to squash merge receipt |
| `mergeToVerifiedMainMs` | p50 `<= 120,000 ms` (`2 min`) | `<= 60,000 ms` (`1 min`) | Squash merge receipt to verified post-merge `main` health |

### Non-resetting clocks, censoring, cohort completeness, human touches, and non-additive stages

- **No clock resets:** Pushing new heads (`headCommits` / `headChangesCount`), converting back to draft and republishing (`publicationEvents` / `redraftCount` / `republishCount`), losing and regaining eligibility (`reeligibilityCount`), or retrying checks (`retryTimestamps` / `retryCount`) never resets `createdAt`, `firstPublishedAt`, or `firstEligibleAt`. All lifecycle timestamps are validated as safe non-negative integers before comparisons so hostile `valueOf` / `Symbol.toPrimitive` objects never execute.
- **Right-censored open work, closed-unmerged PRs, and observation freshness:** Open PRs (`state: 'open'`) and merged PRs awaiting `main` verification are never reported as completed durations; the evaluator records `openAgeMs`, `openDraftAgeMs`, `openPublishedAgeMs`, `openEligibleAgeMs`, `verificationPendingAgeMs`, and `overdueOpenCount` so stalled open work marks `sloMet: false`, and rejects stale open, pending-verification, or `closed-unmerged` observations (`STALE_EVIDENCE`) that lag behind the cohort `observedAt` / `nowMs` freshness bound (since closed-unmerged PRs can reopen). Terminal `closed-unmerged` PRs (`state: 'closed-unmerged'` with explicit `closedAt`) preserve any already completed `draftToPublishMs` measurement while accounting unfinished merge stages separately (`closedUnmergedCount`, `closedUnmergedPullRequests`, `timeToCloseUnmergedMs`) without inflating active open or overdue counts.
- **Reporting-window & cohort completeness (`#18` metric contract):** Every cohort report carries `[fromMs, toMs)`, `observedMs`, `cohort`, and `sourceComplete`. Samples outside `[fromMs, toMs)` fail closed (`SAMPLE_OUTSIDE_WINDOW`), and subsets lacking window metadata or `sourceComplete: true` remain `status: 'partial'` (`reason: 'PARTIAL_COHORT_EVIDENCE'`) with `sloMet: null` and `stretchMet: null` so incomplete subsets never assert `SLO_MET`.
- **Human touches & touch-tracking completeness:** Every sample carries explicit `humanTouchesComplete` (`boolean`). When `humanTouchesComplete === false` (`humanTouches: null` or partial array) and no routine intervention was observed, `humanTouchesCount`, `zeroRoutineHumanTouchesMet`, `unattendedMerge`, and cohort `sloMet` stay `null` (`unknownHumanTouchesCount` / `SLO_INCOMPLETE`) rather than fabricating zero-touch success. Observed routine human interventions (`kind: 'routine'`, including manual or chat-driven merges) mark `zeroRoutineHumanTouchesMet: false`, `unattendedMerge: false`, and `sloMet: false`, while genuine escalations (`kind: 'exception'` across `authorization`, `permission`, `budget`, `conflicting-intent`, or `safety-blocker`) require explicit `attemptedRemediation` and `requiredIntervention`.
- **Non-additive stage attribution:** Waiting is attributed separately across `ciQueueWaitMs`, `linuxRuntimeMs`, `windowsRuntimeMs`, `securityScanMs`, `reviewReworkMs`, `publicationIdleMs`, `eligibilityToEnqueueMs`, `mergeQueueRevalidationMs`, and `mainVerificationMs`. Parallel CI, platform, security, and review stages overlap (`additiveWallClock: false`, `summedWallClockMs: null`), unmeasured stages stay `null` rather than `0`, and velocity objectives never authorize gate bypass, reduced coverage, test skipping, or unapproved spend.
